Oncology therapies are broadly classified into **five major categories** based on their mechanism of action and biological targets: **Cytotoxic Chemotherapy**, **Targeted Therapies**, **Immunotherapies**, **Hormone (Endocrine) Therapies**, and **Cellular & Advanced Therapies**. Below is an overview of the major classes, their sub-classes, mechanism of action, and representative agents. --- ### 1. Traditional Cytotoxic Chemotherapy Cytotoxic chemotherapy predominantly acts by disrupting cell division, DNA replication, or mitosis, targeting rapidly dividing cells. * **Alkylating Agents & Platinum Compounds** * **Mechanism:** Add alkyl groups to DNA bases or create covalent intrastrand/interstrand DNA crosslinks, preventing DNA replication and cell division. * **Sub-classes & Examples:** * *Nitrogen Mustards:* Cyclophosphamide, Ifosfamide, Melphalan, Chlorambucil. * *Nitrosoureas:* Carmustine (BCNU), Lomustine (CCNU). * *Platinum Complexes:* Cisplatin, Carboplatin, Oxaliplatin. * *Triazenes / Methylating Agents:* Temozolomide, Dacarbazine. * **Antimetabolites** * **Mechanism:** Structurally resemble essential endogenous purines, pyrimidines, or folates, incorporating into DNA/RNA or inhibiting synthetic enzymes. * **Sub-classes & Examples:** * *Folate Antagonists:* Methotrexate, Pemetrexed. * *Pyrimidine Analogs:* 5-Fluorouracil (5-FU), Capecitabine, Cytarabine (Ara-C), Gemcitabine. * *Purine Analogs:* Mercaptopurine (6-MP), Fludarabine, Cladribine. * **Mitotic Inhibitors (Microtubule-Targeting Agents)** * **Mechanism:** Disrupt microtubule dynamic instability necessary for mitotic spindle assembly and chromosome segregation. * **Sub-classes & Examples:** * *Taxanes (stabilize microtubules):* Paclitaxel, Docetaxel, Cabazitaxel. * *Vinca Alkaloids (inhibit microtubule polymerization):* Vincristine, Vinblastine, Vinorelbine. * **Topoisomerase Inhibitors** * **Mechanism:** Induce single- or double-strand DNA breaks by stabilizing topoisomerase-DNA cleavage complexes during DNA unwinding. * **Sub-classes & Examples:** * *Topoisomerase I Inhibitors (Camptothecins):* Irinotecan, Topotecan. * *Topoisomerase II Inhibitors (Epipodophyllotoxins):* Etoposide, Teniposide. * **Antitumor Antibiotics** * **Mechanism:** DNA intercalation, generation of free radicals leading to strand scission, or topoisomerase inhibition. * **Sub-classes & Examples:** * *Anthracyclines:* Doxorubicin, Daunorubicin, Epirubicin, Idarubicin. * *Non-anthracyclines:* Bleomycin, Mitomycin C, Dactinomycin. --- ### 2. Targeted Therapies Targeted agents selectively interact with specific oncogenic driver mutations, signal transduction cascades, or cell-surface markers. * **Small Molecule Tyrosine Kinase & Pathway Inhibitors (-ibs)** * **Mechanism:** Competitively bind ATP-binding pockets or allosteric sites of intracellular kinases to block intracellular signaling. * **Key Targets & Examples:** * *BCR-ABL:* Imatinib, Dasatinib, Nilotinib, Ponatinib. * *EGFR:* Erlotinib, Gefitinib, Osimertinib. * *HER2:* Lapatinib, Tucatinib, Neratinib. * *ALK / ROS1:* Crizotinib, Alectinib, Brigatinib, Lorlatinib. * *BRAF / MEK:* Dabrafenib + Trametinib, Encorafenib + Binimetinib. * *BTK:* Ibrutinib, Acalabrutinib, Zanubrutinib. * *VEGFR / Multikinase:* Sunitinib, Sorafenib, Cabozantinib, Lenvatinib. * *KRAS (G12C):* Sotorasib, Adagrasib. * **Non-Kinase Targeted Small Molecules** * **PARP Inhibitors (Synthetic Lethality in HRD/BRCA-mutated cancers):** Olaparib, Rucaparib, Niraparib, Talazoparib. * **Proteasome Inhibitors:** Bortezomib, Carfilzomib, Ixazomib. * **mTOR & PI3K Inhibitors:** Everolimus, Temsirolimus, Alpelisib. * **CDK4/6 Inhibitors (Cell Cycle Regulation):** Palbociclib, Ribociclib, Abemaciclib. * **BCL-2 Inhibitors (Apoptosis Induction):** Venetoclax. * **IDH Inhibitors:** Ivosidenib (IDH1), Enasidenib (IDH2). * **Monoclonal Antibodies (Naked mAbs)** * **Mechanism:** Bind specific extracellular receptors/ligands to inhibit signaling pathways, induce antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC), or prevent angiogenesis. * **Examples:** * *Anti-HER2:* Trastuzumab, Pertuzumab. * *Anti-EGFR:* Cetuximab, Panitumumab. * *Anti-CD20:* Rituximab, Obinutuzumab. * *Anti-VEGF / VEGFR (Anti-angiogenic):* Bevacizumab, Ramucirumab. * *Anti-CD38:* Daratumumab, Isatuximab. * **Antibody-Drug Conjugates (ADCs)** * **Mechanism:** Monoclonal antibodies covalently linked to highly potent cytotoxic payloads (e.g., MMAE, DXd, DM1) for targeted intracellular delivery. * **Examples:** Trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd), Enfortumab vedotin, Sacituzumab govitecan, Brentuximab vedotin. --- ### 3. Immunotherapies Immunotherapies harness or augment the host immune system to recognize and eliminate malignant cells. * **Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors (ICIs)** * **Mechanism:** Block co-inhibitory immune pathways (e.g., PD-1/PD-L1, CTLA-4) to restore T-cell-mediated antitumor activity. * **Sub-classes & Examples:** * *Anti-PD-1:* Pembrolizumab, Nivolumab, Cemiplimab, Dostarlimab. * *Anti-PD-L1:* Atezolizumab, Durvalumab, Avelumab. * *Anti-CTLA-4:* Ipilimumab, Tremelimumab. * *Anti-LAG-3:* Relatlimab. * **Bispecific T-cell Engagers (BiTEs) & Bispecific Antibodies** * **Mechanism:** Dual-targeting construct that simultaneously binds a tumor-associated antigen and an immune receptor (e.g., CD3 on T cells) to bring effector T cells directly into proximity with tumor cells. * **Examples:** Blinatumomab (CD19/CD3), Teclistamab (BCMA/CD3), Elranatamab, Epcoritamab. * **Cytokines & Immunomodulators** * **Recombinant Cytokines:** Interleukin-2 (Aldesleukin), Interferon-alpha. * **Immunomodulatory Drugs (IMiDs):** Lenalidomide, Pomalidomide, Thalidomide (target Cereblon to promote degradation of oncogenic transcription factors). * **Oncolytic Virus & Vaccine Therapies** * **Examples:** Talimogene laherparepvec (T-VEC), Sipuleucel-T. --- ### 4. Hormone (Endocrine) Therapies Used primarily in hormone-sensitive cancers (e.g., ER/PR+ breast cancer, prostate cancer) to inhibit hormone production or block receptor binding. * **Breast Cancer Endocrine Agents** * **Selective Estrogen Receptor Modulators (SERMs):** Tamoxifen, Toremifene. * **Selective Estrogen Receptor Degraders (SERDs):** Fulvestrant, Elacestrant. * **Aromatase Inhibitors (AIs - Non-steroidal/Steroidal):** Anastrozole, Letrozole, Exemestane. * **Prostate Cancer Endocrine Agents** * **GnRH / LHRH Agonists & Antagonists:** Leuprolide, Goserelin, Relugolix, Degarelix. * **Androgen Receptor Pathway Inhibitors (ARPIs):** Enzalutamide, Apalutamide, Darolutamide. * **CYP17A1 Inhibitors (Androgen Synthesis Inhibitors):** Abiraterone acetate. --- ### 5. Cellular, Radiopharmaceutical & Gene Therapies * **Adoptive Cell Therapies (CAR-T / TILs)** * **Chimeric Antigen Receptor (CAR) T-Cell Therapies:** Autologous T cells genetically modified to target surface antigens. * *CD19-targeted:* Tisagenlecleucel, Axicabtagene ciloleucel, Brexucabtagene autoleucel, Lisocabtagene maraleucel. * *BCMA-targeted:* Idecabtagene vicleucel, Ciltacabtagene autoleucel. * **Tumor-Infiltrating Lymphocyte (TIL) Therapy:** Lifileucel. * **Targeted Radiopharmaceuticals (Peptide Receptor Radionuclide Therapy - PRRT / Radioligands)** * **Mechanism:** Delivery of therapeutic ionizing radiation directly to cancer cells expressing specific peptide receptors or antigens. * **Examples:** Lutetium Lu 177 dotatate (SSTR-targeted), Pluvicto / Lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan (PSMA-targeted), Radium-223 dichloride.
